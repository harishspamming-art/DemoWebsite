Claims Center

File a new claim, track an existing one, or learn about our claims process.

📝 How to File a Claim

1

Report the Incident

You can report your incident through any of these channels:

  • Chat: Talk to Ava, our AI assistant, available 24/7
  • Phone: Call 1-800-ACME-INS (Mon-Fri 8AM-8PM, Sat 9AM-5PM EST)
  • Email: Send details to claims@acmeinsurance.demo

Important: Report within 48 hours of the incident for fastest processing.

2

Provide Documentation

Upload or send the following supporting documents:

  • Photos of damage (multiple angles recommended)
  • Police report number (if applicable — required for theft and accidents involving other parties)
  • Receipts for any emergency repairs you've already made
  • Medical bills (if claiming medical payments coverage)
  • Contact information for other parties involved

You can upload documents through your online account or email them to claims@acmeinsurance.demo.

3

Adjuster Assignment

A licensed claims adjuster will be assigned to your case within 24 hours. Your adjuster will:

  • Contact you by phone and email to introduce themselves
  • Review all submitted documentation
  • Schedule an in-person or virtual inspection if needed
  • Provide you with a claim number for tracking
4

Resolution & Payment

Most claims are resolved within 5-10 business days. Once approved:

  • Payment is issued via direct deposit (2-3 business days) or check (5-7 business days)
  • Your applicable deductible will be subtracted from the payout
  • You'll receive a detailed settlement statement via email

⚠️ Important Claims Guidelines

📅 48-Hour Reporting Window

Claims must be filed within 48 hours of the incident. Late claims may still be accepted but could face processing delays.

🔧 Emergency Repairs

If you need to make emergency repairs to prevent further damage (e.g., boarding up a broken window, tarping a damaged roof), go ahead and do so. Keep all receipts — these costs are reimbursable.

🔄 Appeals Process

If your claim is denied, you have 30 days to file an appeal. Submit additional documentation or evidence to your assigned adjuster. Appeals are reviewed within 10 business days, and you'll be notified of the outcome via email and phone.

🚫 Fraud Warning

Fraudulent claims will result in immediate policy cancellation, denial of all pending claims, and referral to law enforcement. Acme Insurance takes fraud seriously to keep premiums fair for all customers.

📞 After-Hours Emergencies

For emergencies outside business hours (car accident, house fire, etc.), call our 24/7 emergency line: 1-800-ACME-911. An emergency claims specialist will assist you immediately.
