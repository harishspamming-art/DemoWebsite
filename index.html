File & Track Claims

How to File a Claim

Step 1: Report the Incident

Chat with our AI assistant Ava, call us at 1-800-ACME-INS, or fill out the online form. Report within 48 hours of the incident for fastest processing.

Step 2: Provide Documentation

Upload photos of damage, police reports (if applicable), and any receipts for related expenses. You can upload documents via your online account or email them to claims@acmeinsurance.demo.

Step 3: Adjuster Assignment

A licensed claims adjuster will be assigned to your case within 24 hours. They will contact you to schedule an inspection if needed.

Step 4: Resolution & Payment

Most claims are resolved within 5-10 business days. Payment is issued via direct deposit or check, minus your applicable deductible.

Claim Status Definitions

StatusMeaning
SubmittedYour claim has been received and is in queue for review.
Under ReviewAn adjuster is evaluating your claim and documentation.
Inspection ScheduledAn in-person or virtual inspection has been arranged.
ApprovedYour claim has been approved. Payment is being processed.
DeniedYour claim was not covered. You may appeal within 30 days.
ClosedClaim has been fully resolved and payment issued.

Important Claim Guidelines

  • Claims must be filed within 48 hours of the incident.
  • Fraudulent claims will result in policy cancellation and legal action.
  • You may appeal a denied claim within 30 days by contacting your adjuster.
  • Emergency repairs (e.g., broken windows, water damage) can be done immediately - keep all receipts for reimbursement.
