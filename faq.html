📋 General Policy Questions

How do I get a quote?

You can get an instant quote by visiting our Plans & Coverage page, chatting with Ava (our AI assistant), or calling us at 1-800-ACME-INS. Online quotes are generated instantly — no appointment needed.

How do I cancel my policy?

You can cancel your policy by calling us at 1-800-ACME-INS or chatting with a human agent. A 30-day written notice is required. Any prepaid premiums will be refunded on a prorated basis. There are no cancellation fees.

Can I change my deductible?

Yes! You can adjust your deductible at any time through your online account or by calling us. Lowering your deductible will increase your monthly premium. Raising your deductible will decrease your monthly premium. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

When does my policy renew?

All Acme Insurance policies are on a 12-month term. Your policy auto-renews 30 days before the expiration date. You'll receive a renewal notice via email 45 days before your renewal date with any premium changes.

Do you offer bundling discounts?

Yes! Bundle any auto plan with any home plan and receive a 15% discount on your total monthly premium. The discount is applied automatically — no promo code needed. See our Plans page for bundle pricing examples.

🚗 Auto Insurance Questions

What's not covered under Auto Shield Basic?

Auto Shield Basic does not cover:

  • Comprehensive events (hail, flood, theft, vandalism, animal strikes)
  • Roadside assistance (towing, flat tire, lockout)
  • Rental car reimbursement
  • Gap coverage

For full coverage, upgrade to Auto Shield Plus at $85/month.

How do I add another driver to my auto policy?

You can add a driver by logging into your online account or calling us at 1-800-ACME-INS. Requirements:

  • The additional driver must be at least 16 years old
  • A driving record check will be conducted
  • Premium adjustments may apply based on the driver's age and record
  • Drivers under 21 may be eligible for our Teen Driver Add-On ($45/month) which includes good student discounts

Does my auto insurance cover rental cars?

Auto Shield Plus includes rental car reimbursement of up to $40/day for up to 14 days while your car is being repaired due to a covered claim. Auto Shield Basic does not include this coverage. For rental cars used during travel (not related to a claim), your existing collision and liability coverage typically extends to rental vehicles within the US.

What do I do if I'm in an accident?

  1. Ensure everyone is safe. Call 911 if there are injuries.
  2. Exchange information with the other driver (name, insurance, license plate).
  3. Take photos of all vehicles, damage, and the scene.
  4. File a police report if there are injuries or significant damage.
  5. Report the incident to Acme Insurance within 48 hours via chat, phone, or email.

🏠 Home Insurance Questions

Does my home insurance cover floods and earthquakes?

The standard Home Guardian plan does not cover flood or earthquake damage. You can add these as separate riders ($35/month for flood, $40/month for earthquake), or upgrade to Home Guardian Premium ($180/month) which includes both.

Does my home insurance cover my home office equipment?

The standard Home Guardian plan covers personal property including electronics, but limits electronics coverage to $2,500. If you work from home and need higher coverage, the Home Guardian Premium plan covers home office equipment up to $10,000 (computers, monitors, desks, printers, office supplies).

Do you cover rental properties?

Yes! We offer a Landlord Protection add-on for any Home plan at $55/month. It covers:

  • Property damage to the rental unit
  • Liability if a tenant or visitor is injured
  • Lost rental income if the property becomes uninhabitable due to a covered event

Contact us for a personalized quote.

What counts as "personal property" under my home plan?

Personal property includes: furniture, clothing, appliances, electronics, jewelry (up to $1,500 — higher limits available), sporting equipment, tools, and other household items. Items must be owned by you or a member of your household. High-value items like art, collectibles, or jewelry over $1,500 may require a separate rider.

💰 Billing & Payment Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
  • Debit cards
  • ACH bank transfer (direct debit)
  • Check (mail to our billing address)

Auto-pay is available and recommended — you'll never miss a payment.

What happens if I miss a payment?

You have a 15-day grace period after your due date. During the grace period, your coverage remains active. If payment is not received within the grace period, your policy will be suspended. You can reinstate your policy within 30 days by paying the overdue balance plus a $25 reinstatement fee. After 30 days, you'll need to apply for a new policy.

Can I pay annually instead of monthly?

Yes! Paying annually gives you a 10% discount compared to monthly billing. Contact us or log into your account to switch to annual billing. The full annual amount is charged at the start of your policy term.
